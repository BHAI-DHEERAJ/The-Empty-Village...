# Local audio

The opening ride, the CRETA pursuit and the optional teashop radio play the uploaded `src/Teri Ramjhol Pari - Masoom Sharma.mp3`. The song starts before the horde catches up, ducks at the tree strike, returns after the restart, and gives way to passing traffic at dawn. The ride starts the song automatically when sound is enabled, with no separate playback buttons. A browser that blocks playback can retry on the next click or key press. The optional teashop radio keeps its own controls. The file is bundled; nothing is streamed from an external service.

The school recorder, roaming Stalker and the chase use bundled Hindi recordings registered in `src/game/audio/VoiceLines.js`. Chase footfalls, the tree and branch strikes, the engine and passing vehicles are synthesized locally. The tape can also be read or replayed from the journal.
